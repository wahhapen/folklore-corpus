# Multilingual folklore vision

This is a direction document, not an executable backlog. Corpus v0.2 remains
bounded to the SKVR and LibriVox vertical slices, deterministic publication,
and a small annotation pilot.

## Destination

Build a provenance-preserving multilingual corpus that lets researchers and
builders compare folklore across Finnic, Slavic, Turkic, and English sources
without replacing originals with translations or flattening culturally
specific entities into universal types.

Possible future capabilities include:

- monolingual, transliteration-aware, and cross-lingual retrieval;
- original/transcript/normalization/transliteration/translation layers linked
  by typed Derivations;
- candidate variant and motif discovery across collections;
- translation comparison with exact original-language citations;
- culture-specific concept pages and a cross-cultural motif atlas;
- multilingual grounded generation that exposes original passages.

These are hypotheses to evaluate after the ingestion and publication path is
proven. They do not create tickets or imply a schedule.

## Non-negotiable research constraints

- No benchmark track exists without a qualified reviewer for its language and
  register.
- Scores are reported by language, direction, collection, and source slice;
  an aggregate must not hide a failed slice.
- Machine translations remain derived Representations and never silently
  replace a captured or human-reviewed text.
- Similarity is a candidate Claim for review, not proof that culture-specific
  beings, motifs, or Witnesses are equivalent.
- Rights discovery precedes adapter work for every post-v0.2 Slavic or Turkic
  collection. The fail-closed rights gate remains authoritative.

## Deferred decisions

The project deliberately does not yet schedule a motif ontology, concept
identifiers, reviewer profiles, a translation-analysis product, cross-lingual
fine-tuning, or a multilingual atlas. The 60-query annotation pilot will
determine whether and how the evaluation plan grows.
